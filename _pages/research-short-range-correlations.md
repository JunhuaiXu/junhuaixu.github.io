  <p class="research-lead">
    Short-range correlations reveal how nucleons behave at small relative distances and high relative momenta inside
    nuclei. My work in this direction connects detector response studies, charged-particle reconstruction, and
    observable extraction in heavy-ion experiments, especially within the CSHINE program.
  </p>

  <h2>Scientific Questions</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>How do correlated nucleon pairs affect measurable reaction observables in finite nuclei?</li>
    <li>Which observables remain robust after detector response, event selection, and reconstruction effects are included?</li>
    <li>How can heavy-ion experimental data constrain the role of short-range correlations in nuclear structure and dynamics?</li>
  </ul>
