  <p class="research-lead">
    Atmospheric neutrinos provide a natural beam for studying neutrino oscillations and detector performance in large
    deep-sea experiments. This research direction focuses on building and validating simulation workflows from flux
    models and neutrino interactions to detector response and event reconstruction.
  </p>

  <div class="research-pill-row">
    <span>Atmospheric neutrinos</span>
    <span>Monte Carlo</span>
    <span>Deep-sea detector</span>
    <span>Event reconstruction</span>
  </div>

  <h2>Scientific Questions</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>How do atmospheric neutrino flux, interaction channels, and detector geometry shape the observable event sample?</li>
    <li>Which detector-response effects dominate the reconstruction of direction, energy, and topology?</li>
    <li>How can simulation be organized so that selection efficiency and systematic uncertainty are transparent?</li>
  </ul>
